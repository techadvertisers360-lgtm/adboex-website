This **local SEO checklist for service businesses** is the same order of work we follow when a plumber, roofer or HVAC company connects to Adboex. It skips the theory and focuses on the steps that most often decide whether you show up in the Google map pack, in regular search results and, increasingly, in answers from AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini.

You don't need to do all 25 steps this week. Work through them in order: the early steps fix the foundations that every later step depends on.

## Why local SEO works differently for service businesses

A coffee shop gets found because people walk past it. A service business usually travels to the customer, which changes how search engines judge you. Google has to understand three things before it will recommend you: **what** you do, **where** you do it and **whether people trust you**. Every item on this checklist strengthens one of those three signals.

Service businesses also face a timing problem. Searches like "emergency plumber" or "roof leak repair" are urgent, and the customer usually calls one of the first three companies they see. Ranking fourth can feel close, but it often brings in a fraction of the calls. That's why consistency matters more than any single trick.

## Part 1: Foundations (steps 1–6)

### 1. Decide on one exact business name, address and phone number

Your name, address and phone number (NAP) should be identical on your website, your Google Business Profile and every directory. Small differences, such as "St." versus "Street" or an old tracking number, create doubt about which details are correct.

### 2. Choose how you show your address

If customers visit your location, show your address. If you only travel to customers, Google lets you hide your address and set a service area instead. Pick the option that matches how you really work.

### 3. Set up Google Search Console

Search Console shows which searches you appear for, which pages Google has indexed and any technical errors. It's free, and it's the most reliable source of truth about your website's search performance.

### 4. Make your website fast on phones

Most local searches happen on mobile. Compress large images, remove plugins you don't use and test your key pages on a real phone over a cellular connection. A slow page loses impatient customers before it ever has a chance to rank.

### 5. Fix crawl errors and broken links

Broken pages, redirect chains and missing sitemaps waste the attention search engines give your site. Run a site audit and fix anything marked as an error before you create new content.

### 6. Add LocalBusiness schema

Schema markup is code that tells search engines your business type, address, hours and service area in a structured way. Adding it helps Google, and AI engines that rely on structured data, understand your business without guessing.

## Part 2: Website content (steps 7–13)

### 7. Create one page per core service

A single "Services" page listing everything rarely ranks for anything. Give each core service its own page: "Water Heater Repair", "Drain Cleaning", "Roof Replacement". Each page should explain the problem, your process, pricing guidance if you can share it, and a clear call to action.

### 8. Create city or service-area pages, carefully

City pages help you rank in the towns you serve beyond your home base. The key is making each page genuinely useful: mention local conditions, neighborhoods you work in, typical jobs and local reviews. Thin pages that only swap the city name tend to perform poorly.

### 9. Put your main keyword in the right places

For each page, choose one main keyword and use it naturally in the title tag, the H1 heading, the first paragraph, one subheading and an image alt text. Don't repeat it unnaturally; clarity beats density.

### 10. Write title tags and meta descriptions that earn clicks

Your title tag (about 50 to 60 characters) and meta description (about 140 to 160 characters) are your ad in the search results. Include the service, the location and a reason to click, such as "same-day service" or "free estimates".

### 11. Answer the questions customers actually ask

Add a short FAQ to every service page. Questions like "How much does a water heater replacement cost?" or "Do you offer emergency service?" match how people search, and they're exactly the kind of content AI assistants quote.

### 12. Make calling you effortless

Put a click-to-call phone number at the top of every page and a short form on every service page. Local SEO only pays off if visitors can contact you in a few seconds.

### 13. Link your pages together

Link each city page to the relevant service pages and back again. Internal links help visitors find what they need and help search engines understand which pages matter most.

## Part 3: Google Business Profile (steps 14–19)

Your Google Business Profile is often the first and only thing a customer sees. Our [complete Google Business Profile optimization guide](/insights/google-business-profile-optimization/) goes deeper, but these are the essentials.

### 14. Pick the most accurate primary category

Your primary category is one of the strongest map pack signals. Choose the one that best matches your main service, then add secondary categories for other services you genuinely offer.

### 15. Complete every field

Hours, services, service areas, attributes, a description and booking links all add clarity. A complete profile looks more trustworthy to both customers and Google.

### 16. Post every week

Weekly posts about recent jobs, seasonal tips and offers show that your business is active. Consistency matters more than perfection.

### 17. Add real photos regularly

Photos of your team, vehicles and finished work build trust and tend to increase engagement. Skip stock photos; customers can tell.

### 18. Ask every happy customer for a review

The number, recency and quality of reviews all influence ranking and, more importantly, whether customers choose you. Make asking a routine part of finishing every job.

### 19. Reply to every review

Thank positive reviewers by name and mention the service. Answer negative reviews calmly and offer to resolve the issue offline. Future customers read your replies as closely as the reviews.

## Part 4: Authority and AI visibility (steps 20–25)

### 20. Get listed in the right directories

Industry directories, your local chamber of commerce and reputable data aggregators reinforce your NAP and your category. Quality beats quantity.

### 21. Earn local mentions and links

Sponsorships, community events, supplier partner pages and local news coverage all build authority. Press distribution can help when you have genuine news to share.

### 22. Track how AI assistants describe you

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity for "the best [service] near [your city]" and note who they recommend. Our guide to [generative engine optimization for local businesses](/insights/generative-engine-optimization-guide/) explains how to improve those answers.

### 23. Track rankings across your service area

A single ranking check from your office tells you little. Geo-grid tracking checks your ranking from many points across town, showing exactly where you win and where competitors take the calls.

### 24. Answer every call and inquiry

Rankings create opportunities; answering converts them. If calls go to voicemail after hours, an [AI receptionist for small business](/insights/ai-receptionist-for-small-business/) can capture those leads around the clock.

### 25. Review results monthly and adjust

Check calls, form submissions, direction requests and rankings every month. Double down on pages and services that bring in work, and fix the ones that don't.

## How long does local SEO take?

Technical fixes and profile improvements can make a difference within weeks. Meaningful ranking and call growth usually builds over 60 to 90 days, depending on how competitive your market is. Be wary of anyone who guarantees a specific ranking: no one controls Google's results, but you can control the signals it uses.
